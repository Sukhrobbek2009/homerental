FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Our support team is available around the clock.

How do I know a host or renter is verified?

Every host and renter completes ID verification before they can list or book. Verified members show a badge on their profile, and cars come with proof of insurance on file.

What does it cost to book a home or car?

Renters never pay to search, message, or create an account. You only pay the listed nightly or daily rate plus a small booking fee, shown in full before you confirm.

Is support really available 24/7?

Yes. Live chat and our phone line are staffed around the clock, every day of the year, in both Uzbek and English. Our office team is also reachable Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm, across all US time zones.

Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?

Most homes offer free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in, and most cars up to 24 hours before pick-up. Exact terms are listed on every listing page before you book.

How do I become a host?

Tap "Become a host," verify your ID, and add photos and details of your home or car. Most listings go live within 24 hours after a quick safety and quality review.

Which cities is Uzbek Rentals available in?

We're active in Brooklyn, Sacramento, Seattle, Chicago, Denver, Philadelphia, and growing — with new cities added as more hosts join the community.
