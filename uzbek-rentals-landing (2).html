Frequently asked questions

Answers to what our community asks most before their first rental.

How does Uzbek Rentals verify hosts and renters?
Every member confirms their identity with a government-issued ID before they can list or book. Hosts with a completed profile and confirmed phone number receive a "Verified" badge visible on every listing.
Is my payment protected if something goes wrong?
Yes. Payments are held securely until 24 hours after check-in or pickup, so funds only release once you've confirmed everything matches the listing. Disputes are reviewed by our trust team within one business day.
What does it cost to list a home or car?
Your first listing is always free. Hosts who want more than one active listing, priority placement, or a featured badge can upgrade to Plus or Pro — see Plans & pricing in the footer for the full breakdown.
Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?
Most listings offer free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in or pickup. Exact terms are set by each host and shown clearly on the listing page before you book.
Do I need to speak Uzbek to use the platform?
Not at all. The platform works fully in English and Uzbek, and you can switch languages anytime from your account settings — many hosts are comfortable in both.
How quickly will support respond if I need help?
Live chat is staffed 24/7 with an average first reply under three minutes. Phone and email support follow the hours listed in the footer below.
